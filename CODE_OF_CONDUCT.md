# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We work to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This repository is a prototype of an adults-only humour product. That is a *subject matter*
decision, not a licence to be cruel to the people building or using it. Raunchy stories
about fictional lads, yes; pile-ons about a real contributor, no.

## Standards

Expectations: using welcoming and inclusive language, respecting differing viewpoints,
accepting criticism gracefully, prioritising the community over being right, and being
explicit when something is a simulation rather than dressing a stub up as a feature.

Unacceptable: sexual attention or advances toward contributors, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and
publishing other people's private information (this one has teeth here: `localStorage`
dumps, message content, and anyone's real name behind a pseudonym are all private).

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the repository owner at
https://github.com/LIN4CRE — every complaint gets reviewed, kept confidential, and answered.
The owner may edit, remove or reject contributions that violate this code, and will say why.

Behaviour that continues after a warning gets a temporary or permanent ban from the repo.
